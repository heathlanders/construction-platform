## Best starting sources
Group baseline: 1926 Subpart Q; UFGS 03/04; NRMCA CIP, ACI free docs, NCMA TEK, BIA Tech Notes

- **`masonry`** — BIA Tech Notes; NCMA TEK; TMS 402/602 (ref); 1926.706
- **`rebar`** — CRSI public placing guides; 1926.701(b) impalement; ACI 318 (ref)
- **`formwork`** — 1926.703 (formwork, public); ACI 347 (ref); UFGS 03 11 13; ICF mfr guides
- **`concrete_pumping`** — ACPA safety manual (American Concrete Pumping Assoc); 1926 Subpart N/O
- **`concrete_cutting`** — CSDA best practices; 1926.1153 silica Table 1
- **`concrete_polishing`** — CPAA guides; 1926.1153 silica
- **`shotcrete`** — ACI 506 (ref); ASA (American Shotcrete Assoc) position statements
- **`post_tensioning`** — PTI field procedures (ref); 1926.701(c) PT safety
